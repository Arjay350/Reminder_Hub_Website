Privacy Policy for Reminder Hub

Effective Date: October 2026 • App Version: v1.4.0 (Android) • Developer: Junjifil Jr. Castro

Local-First Storage

Your app data is stored on your device unless you choose to back it up to Google Drive.

Optional Google Sign-In

Sign in only when you choose to connect Google Drive for cloud backup and restore.

Zero Ads & Trackers

No third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, telemetry, or analytics trackers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Reminder Hub ("the App"), developed by Junjifil Jr. Castro. Reminder Hub is local-first: your app data is stored on your device, and cloud backup is an optional feature that you control.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how your device stores it, and your complete rights and ownership over your personal data.

2. Information Handling & Google Sign-In

You can use Reminder Hub's core features without signing in. If you choose to connect Google Drive for backup or restore, Google sign-in is required to authorize that connection. Google handles your authentication; Reminder Hub does not ask for or store your Google password.

  • Local by default: Data you enter is stored on your device for normal app use.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: When you request a backup or restore, the App uses the Google account and Drive access you authorize to save or retrieve your backup in your Google Drive.
  • No developer-operated backup server: Reminder Hub does not run its own server to store your data. The optional cloud backup is stored in Google Drive, subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.
  • No advertising or sale of app data: The developer does not sell your app data or use it for advertising or cross-service tracking.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use Reminder Hub, content you create is stored in the App's private device storage (powered by local Hive databases):

  • Reminders & Tasks: Titles, due dates, reminder times, tags, and completion status.
  • Timetables & Schedules: Class schedules, subject codes, rooms, and instructor names.
  • Bills & Utility Trackers: Bill amounts, payment deadlines, status indicators, and meter/gas logs.
  • Notebooks & Accounts Vault: Personal notes, checklists, and private credential records secured behind your local PIN or biometric lock.
  • Focus & Pet State: Focus timer statistics, session counts, and virtual pet (Mochi) affinity points.

Local data is protected by Android's application sandbox security model. If you choose to create a Google Drive backup, a copy of the data included in that backup is stored in your Google account as well; creating a backup does not replace the local copy.

4. Android Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

Reminder Hub requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to function as an alarm and reminder tool:

  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to display notification banners and sound alerts when a reminder, scheduled class, or bill reaches its due time.
  • Schedule Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to ensure reminders trigger punctually at the exact second or minute you specify, rather than being delayed by Android battery-saving batching.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used strictly to restore and re-register your scheduled alarms locally if your phone restarts or runs out of battery.
  • Biometric Hardware (USE_BIOMETRIC / Fingerprint): Optional; used exclusively to unlock your protected notes or account vault locally via your device's built-in secure biometric prompt. The App never sees or stores your biometric fingerprints or facial scans.

5. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Reminder Hub uses Google sign-in and Google Drive services when you choose to connect your account for backup or restore. Google processes sign-in and Drive activity under its own terms and privacy policy. Review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Reminder Hub does not integrate with advertising networks or third-party analytics and tracking services such as Google AdMob, Facebook SDK, or Firebase Analytics. If you download the APK from GitHub Releases or another repository, the host platform's own privacy terms apply to the download.

6. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

You maintain 100% control over your data:

  • Editing or Deleting: You can edit or permanently delete any task, note, or entry at any time within the App.
  • Local data: Uninstalling Reminder Hub or clearing its storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Reminder Hub > Clear Storage) removes the App's local database files. This does not delete backup files already saved to Google Drive.
  • Google Drive backups: Delete backup files from your Google Drive to remove cloud copies. You can also review or revoke Reminder Hub's Google account access in your Google Account settings. Google may retain data as described in its own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Reminder Hub's core features store data locally and do not require an account. If Google Drive backup is enabled, Google sign-in and Google's services are subject to Google's terms and age requirements. Parents or guardians should supervise account use where appropriate.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new features are added to Reminder Hub. Any revisions will be published directly on this webpage with an updated effective date.
