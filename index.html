The Idea Behind Reminder Hub

Built to solve a real, everyday struggle

Keeping track of homework, passwords, monthly utility bills, gas refills, and recurring deadlines can quickly become overwhelming when information is scattered across different apps. Reminder Hub was born out of a simple personal need: create one unified, offline-first companion that quietly takes care of the details so nothing slips through the cracks.

App Features

Everything you need to stay on track

Every feature in Reminder Hub was designed for practical utility. Use the tools that match your routine and leave the rest alone.

Daily Organization

Reminders, Calendar & History

Schedule one-off or repeating tasks (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly). Organize by categories like Work, School, Subscriptions, and Personal. Track completed items in full history so you always know when something was finished.

  • Priority levels: Low, Medium, and High
  • Custom reminder lead times & alarm fallbacks
  • Preserved completion records and status tags
Academics & Focus

School Schedules & Study Timer

Keep class schedules, subjects, classrooms, and teacher names in a clean weekly timetable. Jump straight into the integrated Pomodoro Study Timer to power through revision sessions without distraction.

  • Weekly class timetable with day views
  • Pomodoro study timer with subject tracking
  • Safe schedule export & sharing with classmates
Household & Utilities

Bills & LPG Gas Tracker

Stay on top of monthly utility bills with due date notifications, paid occurrence tracking, and payment history. Keep a dedicated log of household LPG cooking gas purchases and monitor tank duration over time.

  • Upcoming, Paid, and Overdue bill statuses
  • Preserved payment history across billing cycles
  • LPG gas consumption and supplier tracking
Reflections & Notes

Diary Notes & Notebooks

Capture quick thoughts, journal entries, or structured notebooks right inside the app. Filter entries by mood tags, browse past thoughts through the mini-calendar, and export notes whenever needed.

  • Structured notebook folders and flexible entries
  • Mood tagging and mini-calendar timeline
  • Full local storage with export capabilities
Credentials & Quotas

Account Manager & AI Resets

Store usernames and passwords behind biometric or PIN security. For developers and AI power users, track usage quotas across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and more, with automatic local reset timers.

  • Secure credential organizer with reveal toggles
  • AI account reset tracking by provider
  • Automatic transition from cooldown to active status
Friendly Companion

Mochi the Pet Companion

Meet Mochi, an animated 2D cat living right on your dashboard. Fully local with zero internet connection required, Mochi reacts to your classes with a backpack, sits with an open book during study, and purrs when pet.

  • 4 customizable coat styles and feeding treats
  • Interactive touch gestures, hearts, and purr audio
  • Naturally celebrates completed tasks & birthdays
Why It Helps

Less mental clutter, more clarity every day

Most productivity apps force you into rigid workflows or require five different services to cover school, home, and personal reminders. Reminder Hub takes the opposite approach.

One calm home for fragmented details

Instead of juggling separate apps for LPG gas, school timetables, bill reminders, and passwords, keep the information you care about under one roof.

100% offline-first reliability

Your reminders, schedules, notes, and credentials live on your device’s local Hive database. The app launches instantly with or without cellular signal.

You control your own backups

Export full encrypted or JSON backups directly to a device folder of your choice, or optionally link Google Drive in Settings for cloud backup peace of mind.

Pick and choose what you use

Are you a student? Focus on School and Study. Managing a household? Use Bills and Gas Tracker. You never need to use every feature to get value.
